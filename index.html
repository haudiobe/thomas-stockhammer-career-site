Thomas Stockhammer

His Dr.-Ing. dissertation, "System and Cross-Layer Design for Mobile Video Transmission", was accepted by Technische Universität München in 2008.[1] As a research assistant at TUM's Institute for Communications Engineering (LNT), he co-authored the publications listed in the institute's publication database.[1] [2] His 2011 ACM MMSys paper "Dynamic adaptive streaming over HTTP: standards and design principles" received the 2021 ACM SIGMM Test of Time Paper Award.[1] [2]

The 3GPP portal lists him as specification rapporteur of TS 26.247 (3GP-DASH) and as work item rapporteur for 23 3GPP work items, from Release 12 to Release 20.[1] [2] [3] [4] He is a co-author of seven IETF RFCs, including RFC 3984 (RTP payload format for H.264 video), RFC 6330 (RaptorQ forward error correction) and RFC 9223 (ROUTE).[1] His recognitions include the INCITS Technical Excellence Award (2013), the 3GPP Excellence Award (2017) and the CTA Technology Achievement Award (2023).[1] [2] [3]
